# Wedding Music Instructions (विवाह संगीत)

You can easily use any wedding song or shehnai music of your choice!

### How to use your own music:
1. Copy your MP3 song into this folder:
   `/home/off-pc/Downloads/ritesh-weds-simran/public/music/`
2. Name the file:
   `shehnai.mp3`
3. That's it! The website will automatically detect and play your custom MP3 file when guests tap the Shehnai button!

### Supported Formats:
- `shehnai.mp3` (Recommended)
- `shehnai.m4a`
- `shehnai.ogg`
